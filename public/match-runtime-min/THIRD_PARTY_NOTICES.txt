THIRD-PARTY NOTICES

HappySeed-derived football Runtime
----------------------------------
The match Runtime interface and portions of the base movement and physics
behavior were adapted from HappySeed open-source components. Targeting 2026
adds its own national-team mapping, 22-player actor layer, pixel players and
kits, stadium presentation, rules, decisions, tactics, substitutions, match
statistics, weather, mobile controls, and React integration. Rights in the
upstream components remain with their respective authors.
